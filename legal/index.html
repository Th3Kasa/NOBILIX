About NOBILIX

NOBILIX PTY LTD is an independent Australian software studio registered in New South Wales. We design, develop, and publish mobile games and applications for iOS and Android. Our current project is TrapMan, a mobile game coming to the Apple App Store and Google Play.

NOBILIX operates as a proprietary limited company under Australian law, regulated by the Australian Securities and Investments Commission (ASIC) and subject to the Australian Consumer Law, the Privacy Act 1988, and all applicable Commonwealth and NSW state legislation.

This Website Privacy Policy describes how NOBILIX PTY LTD collects and uses information when you visit www.nobilix.com. It is separate from the in-game Privacy Policy for TrapMan. If you are looking for TrapMan's privacy policy, see our TrapMan Privacy Policy.

Section 1

Scope of This Policy

This policy applies to the NOBILIX website located at www.nobilix.com (the "Website") and any information collected when you visit, browse, or interact with it. It does not apply to NOBILIX mobile applications — those are governed by their own product-specific privacy policies.

The Website is a static informational site. It does not have user accounts, login functionality, comment systems, or e-commerce. Its primary purpose is to present NOBILIX as a company and provide legal documents for our products.

Section 2

What Information We Collect

a) Information collected automatically

When you visit the Website, your browser may automatically transmit technical information that web servers typically log. This includes:

  • IP address — used to determine approximate geographic region (country-level) for analytics;
  • Browser type and version — e.g., Chrome 124, Safari 17;
  • Operating system — e.g., Windows 11, macOS Sonoma, iOS 17;
  • Referring URL — the page you visited before arriving at our Website;
  • Pages visited and time spent — which pages of the Website you viewed and for how long;
  • Date and time of visit.

This data is collected through standard web server logs and, where applicable, our hosting provider's infrastructure analytics. We do not enrich this data with external sources or use it to identify individuals.

b) Information you provide voluntarily

The Website does not currently include a contact form, newsletter sign-up, or any other input field. If you email us directly at help.nobilix@outlook.com, we will receive your email address and the contents of your message, which we use solely to respond to your inquiry.

c) What we do NOT collect

The Website does not collect: names, payment information, location data, social media profiles, health or biometric data, or any other personal data beyond what is described above. We do not run advertising on the Website, and we do not track visitors across other websites.

Section 3

How We Use This Information

  • Website operation and security: Server logs are used to detect errors, prevent abuse (e.g., DDoS), and ensure the Website remains available;
  • Aggregate analytics: Understanding which pages are visited and from which regions helps us improve the Website's structure and content. We review this data in aggregate — no individual visitor profiles are built;
  • Responding to inquiries: Email addresses provided when you contact us are used solely to reply to your message;
  • Legal compliance: Retaining logs as required by applicable law or to respond to valid legal requests.

We do not use Website data for marketing, advertising, or to build profiles. We do not combine Website visitor data with data from our mobile applications.

Section 5

Cookies & Local Storage

The NOBILIX website does not set any first-party cookies. We do not use cookies for analytics, session management, advertising, or any other purpose.

Google Fonts, served from fonts.googleapis.com, may set cookies in accordance with Google's policies. These are third-party cookies outside our control. You can prevent Google Fonts from loading by disabling JavaScript or using a content blocker, though this will affect the visual appearance of the Website.

We do not display a cookie consent banner because we do not set first-party cookies. If you believe a cookie is being set by our Website that is not accounted for here, please notify us at help.nobilix@outlook.com.

Section 6

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade any information collected through the Website. We may share data in the following limited circumstances:

  • Hosting and infrastructure providers — our hosting provider (Vercel or Netlify) necessarily processes server log data as part of delivering the Website;
  • Legal obligations — if required by law, court order, or government authority, we may disclose information to comply with that obligation;
  • Protection of rights — to protect NOBILIX's rights, property, or safety, or to prevent fraud or abuse.
Section 7

International Transfers

The Website is hosted on infrastructure that may be located in the United States (Vercel or Netlify). If you are accessing the Website from Australia, the EEA, UK, or other jurisdictions, your data may be processed on servers outside your country. Our hosting providers maintain appropriate data processing agreements and transfer safeguards. For EEA/UK visitors, transfers rely on Standard Contractual Clauses.

Section 8

Data Retention

  • Server logs: retained for up to 90 days by our hosting provider, then automatically deleted;
  • Email correspondence: retained for as long as necessary to resolve your inquiry, and for a reasonable period thereafter in case of follow-up. We do not retain emails beyond 2 years unless legally required;
  • Google Fonts request logs: governed by Google's retention policies, not controlled by us.
Section 9

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of personal data we hold about you. Given the minimal data collected through the Website (primarily server logs which are not linked to individual identities), most requests can be satisfied by simply not visiting the Website.

If you have emailed us and wish to request deletion of that correspondence, contact us at help.nobilix@outlook.com from the same email address. We will respond within 30 days.

For jurisdiction-specific rights and supervisory authority contacts, see our TrapMan Privacy Policy, Section 10.

Section 10

The NOBILIX Website is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the Website. The Website contains no interactive features, forms, or sign-up flows that children could use to submit personal information.
