TrapMan — Mobile Game

Data & Compliance

Last updated: 14 May 2026

This page summarises the technical data practices behind Trapman so players, regulators, and platform reviewers can quickly understand what we process and why. It supplements (and does not replace) our Privacy Policy.

Section 1

Data Processors We Use

Service Provider Purpose Data Processed
Authentication Google Firebase Auth Sign-in with Google / Apple / Guest Email, name, account UID
Database Google Cloud Firestore Player profile + leaderboard UID, username, country, scores, level, character, FCM token
Push Notifications Firebase Cloud Messaging Sending in-game notifications FCM device token
Analytics Firebase Analytics Aggregate usage metrics Events, device model, OS, region, Google Ad ID
Payments (Android) Google Play Billing In-app purchases Handled entirely by Google
Payments (iOS) Apple App Store In-app purchases Handled entirely by Apple

We do not integrate any third-party advertising SDK (no AdMob, IronSource, AppLovin, Unity Ads). In-game ads are loaded from URLs we control.

Section 4

Data Location & Transfers

Firebase data may be stored in multi-region data centres operated by Google, including the United States and the European Union. Transfers from the EEA/UK rely on Google's Standard Contractual Clauses. See Google's data processing addendum for details.

Section 6

Account & Data Deletion

You can delete your account directly inside the Game:

1
Trapman → Settings → Account → Delete Account

Deleting your account will:

  • Remove your profile from Firestore (users/{uid}).
  • Anonymise leaderboard entries (your score may remain but no longer linked to a username).
  • Invalidate your FCM token.
  • Clear local data (PlayerPrefs).

Alternatively, email help.nobilix@outlook.com from your account email and we will action the request within 30 days, in line with GDPR Art. 17 / CCPA §1798.105 / Australian Privacy Principle 11.

Section 7

Children's Privacy

Trapman is not designed for children under 13 (under 16 in the EEA/UK). We do not implement COPPA-compliant flows. If we learn that a child's data has been collected, it will be deleted.

Section 8

Security Measures

  • TLS 1.2+ in transit (enforced by Firebase SDKs).
  • Encryption at rest on Google Cloud infrastructure.
  • Firestore Security Rules limit each user to read/write only their own profile.
  • No payment card data ever touches our systems.
