Section 8

Account & Data Deletion

Method 1 — In-app (immediate)

1
Open TrapMan and sign in to your account.
2
Tap Settings (gear icon) from the main menu.
3
Tap Account, then tap Delete Account.
4
Confirm deletion when prompted. The action is irreversible.

Deleting your account will:

  • Hard-delete your profile document from Firestore (users/{uid}) within 30 days;
  • Anonymise your leaderboard entries (score data retained, username and UID removed) within 30 days;
  • Invalidate and delete your FCM push token;
  • Clear local PlayerPrefs data on the device;
  • Revoke Firebase Authentication session.

Purchase entitlement records are retained in anonymised form for the legally required period (7 years) but are no longer linked to your identity.

Method 2 — Email request

Email help.nobilix@outlook.com from the email address associated with your account with the subject line "Account Deletion Request". We will verify your identity and process the deletion within 30 days, in accordance with:

  • GDPR Art. 17 (Right to Erasure);
  • CCPA §1798.105 (Right to Delete);
  • Australian Privacy Principle 11.2 (Destruction and De-identification).

Section 10

Data Breach Notification Policy

In the event of a personal data breach, NOBILIX will:

  • Within 72 hours of becoming aware: notify the relevant supervisory authority (OAIC in Australia; lead DPA in EEA; ICO in UK) where the breach is likely to result in a risk to the rights and freedoms of individuals, as required by GDPR Art. 33, UK GDPR Art. 33, and the Australian Notifiable Data Breaches scheme (Privacy Act s 26WK);
  • Without undue delay: notify affected individuals directly (via in-app notification and/or email) where the breach is likely to result in a high risk to their rights and freedoms, including the nature of the breach, the data affected, likely consequences, and steps we are taking;
  • Immediately: contain and mitigate the breach, and document it in our internal breach register regardless of whether notification is required.

To report a suspected breach or vulnerability affecting our users, email help.nobilix@outlook.com with subject "Data Breach Report". We aim to acknowledge within 5 business days.

Section 11

Children's Privacy

TrapMan is rated for users aged 13 and over (16 in the EEA/UK). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the applicable minimum age. The Game requires authentication via a Google or Apple account, both of which enforce minimum age requirements during account creation.

We do not implement age-gating or COPPA-specific data flows beyond relying on Google and Apple's age controls. If we receive credible notice that a child under the applicable minimum age has provided us with personal data, we will delete that data within 30 days. Notices should be sent to help.nobilix@outlook.com with the subject "Child Privacy Concern".

Applicable frameworks: US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA); GDPR Art. 8 (age of digital consent); Australian Privacy Act (children's data).

Section 13

Privacy by Design

We apply privacy-by-design principles throughout the development of TrapMan:

  • Data minimisation: We collect only the data strictly necessary for each feature. Guest mode collects no personal data beyond an anonymous device ID.
  • Purpose limitation: Data collected for one purpose (e.g., game save) is not repurposed for unrelated activities (e.g., advertising profiling).
  • Storage limitation: Retention periods are defined and enforced for each data category (see Section 7).
  • No third-party ad SDKs: We deliberately excluded all third-party advertising and attribution SDKs to minimise data sharing with advertising networks.
  • Firestore security rules: Written with least-privilege access — each user can only access their own data; no authenticated user can list all users or read another's profile.
  • In-app deletion: Users can delete their account and all associated data directly from within the Game without contacting support.
  • No profiling: We do not build individual behavioural profiles or use analytics data to make decisions about individual users.

Section 15

Vulnerability & Privacy Concern Reporting

If you discover a security vulnerability or privacy issue affecting TrapMan or NOBILIX's systems, please report it responsibly:

  • Email: help.nobilix@outlook.com
  • Subject line: "Security Vulnerability" or "Privacy Concern"
  • Include: a description of the issue, steps to reproduce, affected component, and any evidence (screenshots, logs)
  • Response time: We aim to acknowledge within 5 business days and provide a remediation timeline within 14 business days

We do not operate a formal bug bounty programme at this time, but we are grateful for responsible disclosures and will acknowledge reporters (with their permission) in our internal changelog.

Please do not publicly disclose vulnerabilities before we have had a reasonable opportunity to investigate and remediate.
