NOBILIX PTY LTD ("Nobilix", "we", "us", "our") publishes the mobile game Trapman (the "Game"). This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect when you use the Game, why we collect it, how we use it, and the rights available to you. By installing or using Trapman, you confirm you have read and agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not install or use the Game.

Section 2

Information We Collect

a) Information you provide directly

  • Sign-in with Google (Android) or Apple (iOS): your display name or username, email address, and a unique account identifier (UID) issued by Google or Apple. We receive only what those platforms share with us; we do not receive your Google or Apple password.
  • In-game profile: the Username you choose and your country of residence (defaults to AU; you may change it at any time).
  • Guest mode: if you select "Continue as Guest" we generate a random anonymous user ID tied to your device. No name or email is collected or linked.
  • In-app purchase confirmation: when you buy Remove Ads, Music Unlock, Item Pack, or Continue Hearts, the payment is processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We receive only a confirmation that entitlement has been granted to your UID — we never receive, see, or store your payment card details, billing address, or any financial information.

b) Information collected automatically when you play

  • Game progress: current character, current level, and high score, synced to your account in Firestore.
  • Leaderboard data: username, country flag (ISO code), score, and submission timestamp, stored and displayed publicly so other players can see rankings.
  • Device and technical data: device model, operating system version and build number, app version, preferred language, and approximate geographic region (country-level, derived from IP by Firebase — the precise IP is not stored by us). On Android, the Google Advertising ID (GAID) is also collected by Firebase Analytics.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) token: a push-notification identifier generated by Firebase and stored against your account. Used to deliver in-game notifications such as event announcements and daily reminders.
  • Analytics events: standard behavioural events emitted by the Firebase Analytics SDK — for example, session_start, level_up, purchase, and app_remove. These are aggregated to help us understand gameplay and improve the experience; we do not build individual profiles from analytics data.

c) Information stored only on your device (never sent to us)

  • Audio settings: Music, SFX, and Haptics on/off toggles (stored in PlayerPrefs).
  • Locally cached high score.
  • Google Sign-In and Apple Sign-In session tokens, managed by the respective platform SDK.

d) What we do NOT collect

We do not collect: precise GPS location, contacts or address book, photos or camera, microphone or audio input, SMS or call logs, biometric data (fingerprint, face ID), health data, financial account numbers, government ID numbers, race or ethnicity, religious or political beliefs, or any special/sensitive categories of personal data under GDPR Article 9.

Section 4

How We Use Your Information

  1. Account creation and authentication — creating and securing your player account so your progress is linked to you across devices.
  2. Game delivery and progress sync — saving and restoring your level, character, and score so you can continue where you left off.
  3. Public leaderboards — displaying your username, country, and score to all players on global and country-filtered leaderboard views.
  4. In-app purchase verification — confirming that an entitlement (e.g., Remove Ads) has been granted to your account by Google Play or the Apple App Store.
  5. Advertising display — showing banner and interstitial ads to users who have not purchased Remove Ads. Ads are served from URLs we control; no third-party ad SDK has access to your data.
  6. Push notifications — sending you in-game messages (event announcements, daily reminders) where you have granted notification permission.
  7. Service improvement and analytics — using aggregate, anonymised analytics data to understand which levels players find difficult, which features are most used, and how to improve gameplay balance.
  8. Security and fraud prevention — detecting cheating, manipulated scores, or unauthorised access to accounts.
  9. Legal compliance — fulfilling our obligations under the Australian Privacy Act, GDPR, CCPA, and other applicable law.

We do not use your personal information for any purpose not listed above without first notifying you and, where required, obtaining your consent.

Section 6

Advertising

Trapman displays banner and interstitial advertisements in the free version of the Game. Key facts:

  • Ads are served from image/video URLs hosted or approved by us — not from a third-party ad network SDK running inside the app.
  • Ads are not personalised based on your identity, browsing history, or third-party data profiles.
  • Tapping an ad opens the destination URL in your device's default browser. From that point, the third party's own privacy policy applies and we have no control over or responsibility for their data practices.
  • Purchasing the Remove Ads in-app product permanently disables all banner and interstitial advertisements on your account across all devices where you are signed in.
Section 7

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share it only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Google Firebase (Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd): Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Firebase Cloud Messaging, and Firebase Analytics. Google processes data as our data processor under a Data Processing Agreement. Data may be processed in the United States and other countries where Google operates. See Google's Firebase Privacy information.
  • Google Play Billing (Google LLC): handles payment processing for Android in-app purchases. We receive only a purchase confirmation token.
  • Apple App Store (Apple Inc.): handles payment processing for iOS in-app purchases. We receive only a receipt validation response.
  • Other players (public leaderboards): your username, country flag, and score are visible to all players in the Game. Do not use a username that reveals your real identity if you wish to remain anonymous.
  • Legal authorities: we may disclose your information if required by applicable law, enforceable court order, or government request; or to protect the rights, property, or safety of NOBILIX, our players, or the public.
  • Business transfers: if NOBILIX is involved in a merger, acquisition, restructuring, or sale of assets, personal information may be transferred to the successor entity, subject to the same privacy protections. We will notify you via in-app notice before any such transfer.

We do not share your information with advertisers, data brokers, marketing platforms, or social media networks.

Section 17

Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorised disclosure, or access. Specific measures include:

  • TLS 1.2+ encryption in transit — enforced by the Firebase SDK for all communication between the Game and Google's servers.
  • Encryption at rest — provided by Google Cloud's infrastructure for Firestore and Firebase services.
  • Firestore Security Rules — restrict each authenticated user to read and write only their own profile document (users/{uid}). No user can access another user's data.
  • No payment data — payment card details never touch our systems or servers; all payment processing is handled externally by Google Play and Apple App Store.
  • Access control — Firebase console access is limited to authorised NOBILIX personnel only, protected by multi-factor authentication.

No method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your personal information, we cannot guarantee absolute security. We recommend using a strong, unique password for your Google or Apple ID.
