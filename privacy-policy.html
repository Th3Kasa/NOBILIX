Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 May 2026  ·  Last updated: 14 May 2026

NOBILIX PTY LTD ("Nobilix", "we", "us", "our") publishes the mobile game Trapman (the "Game"). This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect when you use the Game, why we collect it, how we use it, and the choices you have. By installing or using Trapman, you agree to the practices described here.

Section 1

Who We Are

  • Controller: NOBILIX PTY LTD
  • Contact: help.nobilix@outlook.com
  • ABN/ACN: 65 689 067 981
Section 2

Information We Collect

a) Information you provide

  • Account information when you sign in with Google (Android) or Apple (iOS): your name/username, email address, and a unique account identifier provided by Google/Apple.
  • Profile information you set inside the Game: display name (Username) and country of residence (defaults to AU; you can change it).
  • Guest mode: if you choose "Continue as Guest" we generate an anonymous user ID — no name or email is collected.
  • Purchase requests: when you buy in-app items (Remove Ads, Music Unlock, Item Pack, Continue Hearts), the transaction is processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We receive a confirmation that ownership is granted to your account but we do not receive or store your payment details.

b) Information collected automatically

  • Game progress: current character, current level, high score.
  • Leaderboard data: your username, country, score, and timestamp are uploaded so other players can see ranks.
  • Device & technical data: device model, operating system version, language, app version, Google Advertising ID (Android), and approximate region — collected by Google Firebase services to operate the Game.
  • Push notification token (FCM token): a Firebase-generated identifier used to send you in-game notifications. Stored against your account so we can send you messages (e.g., new events, daily reminders).
  • Analytics events: standard Firebase Analytics events (e.g., session start, level completed, purchase events). Used to understand aggregate usage and improve the Game.

c) Information stored only on your device

  • Audio preferences (Music, SFX, Haptics on/off)
  • Locally cached high score
  • Sign-in tokens managed by the Google/Apple SDK

We do not collect: precise GPS location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, SMS, call logs, or biometric data.

Section 3

How We Use Your Information

We use the information above to:

  1. Create and maintain your player account and game progress.
  2. Display global and country-based leaderboards.
  3. Process and verify in-app purchases.
  4. Show ads inside the Game (banner + interstitial) and to honour the "Remove Ads" purchase.
  5. Send push notifications you have opted into.
  6. Detect bugs, prevent fraud/cheating, and improve gameplay.
  7. Comply with legal obligations.

Legal bases (GDPR / UK GDPR users): performance of contract (running the Game and your account), legitimate interests (improving and securing the service, basic analytics), consent (push notifications, optional analytics/ad personalisation where required by law), and legal obligation.

Section 4

Advertising

Trapman shows in-game advertisements served from URLs hosted by us. Ads are not personalised based on third-party tracking SDKs. If you tap an ad, your device's browser will open the destination URL — that third party's privacy policy then applies. Buying the "Remove Ads" product permanently disables banner and interstitial ads on your account.

Section 5

Sharing Your Information

We share information only with:

  • Google Firebase (Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd) — Authentication, Firestore database, Cloud Messaging, Analytics, hosting. Data may be processed in the United States and other regions where Google operates.
  • Google Play Billing / Apple App Store — payment processing for in-app purchases.
  • Other players — your username, country, and score are visible on public leaderboards. Do not use a username that identifies you personally if you wish to remain anonymous.
  • Authorities when required by law, court order, or to protect our rights or user safety.

We do not sell your personal information.

Section 6

International Data Transfers

Firebase processes data on servers operated by Google, including in the United States and the European Union. Where required (e.g., for EEA/UK users), transfers rely on Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission and equivalent UK mechanisms.

Section 7

Data Retention

  • Account data and game progress: retained while your account is active.
  • Leaderboard scores: retained indefinitely so historical rankings remain meaningful (anonymised on account deletion).
  • FCM token: refreshed automatically; old tokens deleted when invalidated.
  • Local PlayerPrefs: cleared when you log out or uninstall the Game.
Section 8

Your Rights

Depending on your country (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, Australian Privacy Act, etc.) you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction or deletion.
  • Object to or restrict processing.
  • Withdraw consent (e.g., turn off notifications in your device settings).
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (in Australia, the OAIC).

To exercise any right, email help.nobilix@outlook.com from the email tied to your account, or use the in-app "Delete Account" option (Settings → Account). We respond within 30 days.

Section 9

Children

Trapman is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA/UK). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Section 10

Security

We rely on Google Firebase's encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest. No system is 100% secure; please use a strong account password with your Google/Apple ID.

Section 11

Changes

We may update this Policy. The "Last updated" date will change and material updates will be announced inside the Game.
