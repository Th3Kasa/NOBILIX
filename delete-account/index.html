Deletion is permanent. Once your account and data are deleted, this cannot be undone. Your game progress, leaderboard scores, unlocked characters, and purchase history will be gone for good.

Section 1

What Gets Deleted

When we process your deletion request, we permanently remove:

  • Your account and player profile
  • Game progress, high scores, and leaderboard entries
  • Unlocked characters, skins, and power-ups
  • Any in-app purchase records held on our servers
  • Device identifiers and push notification tokens linked to your account
  • Any support correspondence associated with your account

Deleting your account does not automatically cancel any active subscriptions or trigger refunds for past purchases. Refund requests must be made directly through the App Store or Google Play.

Section 2

How to Request Deletion

Send an email to help.nobilix@outlook.com from the email address linked to your TrapMan account. This is how we verify the request is coming from you.

Use the template below — copy it, fill in your details, and hit send. We'll take it from there.

Your request must come from the email address registered to your account. Requests from a different address can't be verified and will not be processed.

Section 4

What Happens Next

Here's what to expect after you send your request:

  • We receive your request
    We'll send an acknowledgement email within 2 business days confirming we've received it.
  • We verify your identity
    We check that the email matches the account on record. If we need any clarification, we'll reach back out.
  • Your data is deleted
    All personal data is permanently deleted within 7 business days of verification. You'll receive a confirmation email when it's done.
Section 5

What We May Retain

In limited cases, some information may be kept after your account is deleted:

  • Legal obligations — if we're required by law to keep certain records (e.g. financial transaction records under Australian tax law), those will be retained for the legally required period only;
  • Fraud prevention — if your account was flagged for abuse or fraudulent activity, we may retain minimal identifying information to prevent re-registration;
  • Aggregated, non-identifiable data — anonymised gameplay statistics that can't be traced back to you may be retained for analytics purposes.

Any retained data is held only for as long as necessary for the specific purpose, then deleted.
